Terms of Service

Effective: 25 November 2026

1. Intended use

This is a private, owner-operated backup and recovery utility for JDC COUNTINGS, not a general public storage service. Authorization is limited to the designated owner or an expressly authorized administrator. You must have permission to access the source database and destination account.

2. Google Drive authorization

The utility uses the limited Drive permission described in its Privacy Policy. Authorization may be revoked in Google Account settings at any time. The owner must safeguard credentials and avoid granting unnecessary permissions.

3. Recovery responsibilities

The owner must keep secure independent copies of the recovery key and its passphrase, check backup integrity, review access permissions, and verify restoration in an isolated environment. Loss of the recovery key may make encrypted archives unrecoverable. Backups must not be restored over a live database as an unapproved test.

4. Availability and limitations

Backup operations depend on Google Drive, GitHub Actions, the database service, and network availability. Backup creation alone does not guarantee full recoverability. The utility is not a guarantee of uninterrupted service or a specified recovery time. Production activation requires successful validation and owner approval.

5. Support and changes

Contact the developer support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen or the JDC Onlinecenter maintainer for assistance. Changes to these terms will be published here.
